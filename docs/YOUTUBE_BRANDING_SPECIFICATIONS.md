# YouTube Branding Specifications

No finished channel art was generated in RC27 because approved LifeTalk-specific logos, stills, and trailer footage were not available in `public/media/lifetalk/`. This specification is production-ready and must be executed only with verified official assets.

## Shared Production Standards

- Master channel banner: 2560 x 1440 px.
- Critical mobile-safe content area: 1235 x 338 px, centred within the master.
- Profile logo working file: 800 x 800 px, designed to remain legible as a circular crop.
- Video watermark: transparent PNG, 150 x 150 px minimum; simple silhouette and high contrast.
- Thumbnail master: 1280 x 720 px, 16:9, under YouTube's current file-size limit.
- Shorts cover working file: 1080 x 1920 px with critical text and subject inside the central mobile-safe region.
- Export colour: sRGB.
- Keep all editable masters layered and preserve a clean, text-free background version.
- Check banners on mobile, desktop, and TV before publication.
- Use a maximum of 3-5 thumbnail words; never add fake awards, statistics, or misleading claims.

## LifeTalk TV System

- Character: cinematic, emotional, inspirational, human-centred, credible.
- Palette: deep black, warm off-white, controlled red, and restrained gold.
- Banner copy: `LifeTalk TV` and `Stories That Inspire Life` only.
- Primary subject: one verified, emotionally clear subject or scene; avoid collages unless the composition remains readable in the safe area.
- Profile: approved LifeTalk mark with no small tagline text.
- Watermark: single-colour approved LifeTalk mark.
- Trailer thumbnail: one strong verified frame plus `Stories That Inspire Life`.
- Movie/series template: cinematic frame, title, optional verified episode number, logo in a fixed corner.
- Interview template: subject portrait, name or topic in 3-5 words, clean negative space.
- News/community template: credible documentary frame, precise topic, no sensational arrows or fabricated urgency.
- Playlist covers: consistent title band and one approved category image.

## BaBra TV System

- Character: premium, entrepreneurial, corporate, visionary, African, trustworthy.
- Palette: BaBra black, gold, white, with one approved division accent where needed.
- Banner copy: `BaBra TV` and `Building a Better Future` only.
- Profile: official BaBra mark optimized for circular display.
- Watermark: simplified official BaBra mark in white or gold.
- Corporate trailer thumbnail: verified founder or ecosystem image plus `Building a Better Future`.
- Corporate template: clear official subject, black/gold frame, short title, consistent logo position.
- Product template: approved product image only, division label, concise factual title.
- Project template: verified project media, clear division label, no unverified completion claims.
- Division playlist covers: one system with controlled colour accents for Cosmetics, Mobile Hub, Schools, and Foundation.

## Thumbnail QA

- Confirm the image is official and publication rights are documented.
- Confirm the title and thumbnail make the same promise.
- Check legibility at 10% scale and on a phone.
- Avoid faces or products obscured by timestamps.
- Confirm spelling, episode numbering, and division labels.
- Remove excessive text, logos, borders, arrows, emoji, and decorative clutter.
