# YouTube 90-Day Content Plan

This is a recommended sustainable plan, not a guaranteed publishing commitment. Publish only content that is complete, rights-cleared, fact-checked, and approved.

## Recommended Rhythm

### LifeTalk TV

- One major episode, film-related upload, documentary, or substantial story each week.
- Two Shorts each week, derived from approved source footage.
- One interview or inspirational feature every two weeks.
- Community posts between uploads when there is a genuine update, question, or story to share.

### BaBra TV

- One company or founder update each week.
- Two Shorts each week using verified BaBra footage.
- One project or division feature every two weeks.
- Community posts for verified announcements and milestones.

## Weekly Production Pattern

- Monday: confirm approvals, factual accuracy, title direction, rights, and audience setting.
- Tuesday: edit major upload and prepare captions.
- Wednesday: thumbnail review, metadata, playlist, cards, and end screen.
- Thursday: final approval and schedule.
- Friday or weekend: publish at the time supported by channel analytics.
- Between major uploads: publish approved Shorts and purposeful community posts.

## Measurement Without Vanity Claims

Review privately in YouTube Studio:

- Click-through rate by content type and thumbnail system.
- Average view duration and key retention changes.
- Returning viewers and traffic sources.
- Playlist starts and continuation.
- Comments requiring response or moderation.
- Subtitle usage and audience geography where reliable.

Do not publish internal performance figures unless leadership verifies and approves them.
