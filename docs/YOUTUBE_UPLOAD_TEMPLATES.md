## Pre-publication Checklist

- Confirm title case; avoid all caps and excessive emojis.
- Verify every name, claim, date, episode number, product detail, and project status.
- Confirm music, footage, image, logo, location, and contributor rights.
- Add the correct verified playlist.
- Add accurate language and subtitles.
- Review audience setting individually; do not default the entire channel to Made for Kids.
- Review thumbnail accuracy and mobile readability.
- Add appropriate cards and end screens using published content only.
- Complete copyright checks.
- Check comment, remix, chapters, and visibility settings.
- Obtain final editorial or corporate approval before scheduling.
