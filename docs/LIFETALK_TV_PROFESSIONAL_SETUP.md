# LifeTalk TV Professional Setup

## Positioning

**Channel:** LifeTalk TV

**Current handle:** `@lifetalkt`

**Tagline:** Stories That Inspire Life

LifeTalk TV is the entertainment and human-story channel in the BaBra ecosystem. It should feel cinematic, emotional, credible, and human-centred. Corporate division updates belong on BaBra TV unless the content is developed as a genuine documentary, interview, or human-interest feature.

## Channel Trailer Brief

- Target length: 45-60 seconds.
- Open with the strongest verified emotional image and natural sound.
- Move through verified film or series clips, interview moments, human stories, community scenes, and Nzabigeraho material.
- Use a concise voice-over: who LifeTalk TV is, what audiences can expect, and why the stories matter.
- End with a clear invitation to subscribe.
- Closing card: **LifeTalk TV - Stories That Inspire Life**.
- Do not fabricate footage; build the edit only after official clips are cleared.

## Audience Review

- Do not classify the entire channel as Made for Kids.
- Review every upload individually based on its intended audience, language, topic, presentation, and child-directed elements.
- The appearance of children alone does not determine the setting.
- Record the decision and reviewer for each upload.

## Professional Settings Checklist

- Configure comment moderation and review blocked-word rules regularly.
- Use named channel permissions; do not share account passwords.
- Require two-step security for channel managers.
- Review subtitles and language metadata before publishing.
- Review automatic chapters for accuracy.
- Decide remix permissions video by video.
- Moderate community posts and live chat.
- Complete copyright checks before publication.
- Confirm verification status inside YouTube Studio.
