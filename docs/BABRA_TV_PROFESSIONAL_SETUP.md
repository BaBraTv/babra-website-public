# BaBra TV Professional Setup

## Positioning

**Channel:** BaBra TV

**Current handle:** `@babratv`

**Tagline:** Building a Better Future

BaBra TV is the official corporate media channel of the BaBra ecosystem. It documents leadership, company development, products, projects, innovation, education, community impact, and behind-the-scenes work. Narrative entertainment and general human-interest programming belong on LifeTalk TV.

## Audience and Governance

- Do not set the whole channel as Made for Kids.
- Review every upload separately and document the decision.
- Use Brand Account permissions and least-privilege access.
- Require two-step security for every manager.
- Verify factual claims and obtain approvals for company announcements before scheduling.
- Complete rights, privacy, music, product-claim, and copyright checks before publication.
